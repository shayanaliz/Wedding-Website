# Wedding site (index)

Scope: the one-page site. Mode: Experience leaning Persuade; guests from WhatsApp, on phones. Action: know both events, get directions, save the dates.
Structure comes from the reference in references/; theme is pinned by the user to the invitation (palette, fonts, pattern, art). No kickers, RSVP or wishes.
Story and photos section is added when the material arrives. Music is optional and waits on the client.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the couple's invitation card opened out into one long sheet; it refuses a floral hero sitting over rows of equal cards.
OWN-WORLD: Cream #FFFBEE with the mandala lattice at 12%; dark blue rule frames with the invitation's flower corners; whole fields of pink, dark blue and yellow; Vienna Romance names; Libre Baskerville caps, italic and regular.
STORY: Guests are welcomed by the monogram, open the card, learn the two events, reach directions and calendar in a tap, meet both families, keep the invitation.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen framed card, flower corners on all four, crown flower on the top rule, the AS monogram tracing itself large at centre, date line beneath, solid dark blue "Open our invitation" below.
FORM: User-pinned direction, position 1. seed: none (user-pinned direction). Signature interaction: the card's two halves part like doors to reveal the page. Motion: monogram trace, doors, script headings wipe on as if written, light drift on the photo.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
